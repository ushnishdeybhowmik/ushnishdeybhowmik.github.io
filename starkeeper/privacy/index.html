Star Keeper – Privacy policy

Effective 14 November 2026 · App: Star Keeper: Mahjong Solitaire (com.udb.starkeeper) · Developer: Ushnish Dey Bhowmik · Contact: neilbhowmik05@gmail.com

In short: The game runs on your phone, and your progress, scores and settings stay on your phone. The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data, described below. The app has no analytics or crash-reporting service of its own, no account and no sign-in, and we never sell data.

1. Your data in the game

  • Your progress (which constellations you have restored), stardust, hints, Relic charges, streaks, the history of boards you cleared and your settings are stored only on your phone. We cannot see them.
  • A board in progress is saved on your phone so you can continue it later.
  • Board codes ("Challenge a friend") name a board, such as ORION-7F3K. A code holds only which board it is, never anything about you. Sharing one uses Android's share menu, so you choose where it goes; nothing is sent by the app itself.
  • If you choose a sign under "Your sign", only the sign is kept, on your phone. We never ask for a birth date.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of this.

2. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: only for ads. The game itself works fully offline.
  • Notifications: for the optional daily reminder (off until you turn it on), at most one a day. You can turn it off any time in Settings.
  • Run at startup, wake lock and foreground service: so the reminder is scheduled again after your phone restarts and arrives on time (Android's WorkManager).
  • Advertising ID and Android's ad-services permissions (Privacy Sandbox): added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK, used as described in section 3.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: a short ad after some cleared boards (never in your first session or before your fifth board, and only when the button says "short ad"), an ad when you return to the app (not on your first day, at most once every 4 hours, and never over a board), optional ads you choose to watch for an extra (a shuffle, hints, a Relic charge, a star back, double stardust, a streak restore, the next event board, or a day's trial of a tile style or sky theme), and a banner on the Atlas and Events screens. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID, and Android Privacy Sandbox signals (Topics and ad measurement) where your phone supports them;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location (country or city) is inferred;
  • device and app information, and how you interact with ads.

This is used for showing ads, ad measurement, frequency capping and preventing fraud. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland or a region with similar laws, the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before loading any ad, and you can change your choice any time in Settings › Privacy choices for ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Ratings and crash reports through Google Play

  • Once, after you restore your third constellation, the app may show Google Play's own rating card. Your rating goes to Google Play, not to the app.
  • The app has no crash-reporting service. If you allow your phone to share usage and diagnostics with Google, Google Play may report crashes to us in anonymous form (Android vitals), under Google's privacy policy.

6. Children

The game is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. Your choices

  • Change your ad consent in Settings › Privacy choices for ads (where offered).
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google or privacy settings.
  • Turn the daily reminder off in Settings › Play.
  • Uninstall the app to delete everything it keeps on your phone.
  • Questions or a data request: email neilbhowmik05@gmail.com. Because there is no account, include the date you used the app so we can help.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Important changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

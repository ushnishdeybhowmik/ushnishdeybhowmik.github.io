ID Photo Maker – Privacy policy

Effective 12 November 2026 · App: ID Photo Maker (com.udb.idphoto) · Developer: Ushnish Dey Bhowmik · Contact: neilbhowmik05@gmail.com

In short: your photos and signatures are processed on your phone and are never uploaded by us. The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. It uses Google Firebase to count how features are used and to report crashes. These Google services collect some device data, described below. There is no account and we never sell data.

1. Your photos and signatures

  • Cropping, resizing, file-size fitting, face checks, background change and signature cleanup all run on your phone. We do not upload, store or see your photos.
  • While you work, the app keeps temporary copies in its private storage. These are deleted automatically within 24 hours.
  • Photos you save go to your phone's gallery (Pictures › ID Photos), and print-sheet PDFs to Downloads › ID Photos. They stay there until you delete them.
  • The "My photos" list (form name, file name, size and date of each saved photo) is stored only on your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

2. Permissions

  • Camera: to take the photo. Only used while the camera screen is open.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only): to save the finished photo to your gallery. Newer Android versions need no permission for this.
  • Picking a photo uses Android's photo picker, so the app only sees the photo you choose.
  • Internet: for ads, for Firebase, and to download Google's on-device image models the first time they are needed.

3. Face detection and background change (Google ML Kit)

Face detection and finding the person for a background change use Google ML Kit through Google Play services. The models run on your phone and your images are not sent to Google. Google may collect basic information about how the ML Kit features perform (for example device model, app version and timings) to maintain and improve them. See the ML Kit terms.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a short ad after saving (not on your first save), optional ads you choose to watch to unlock an extra (background change, signature cleanup, print sheet), and a banner at the bottom of the form lists and the My photos screen. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID, and Android Privacy Sandbox signals (Topics and ad measurement) where your phone supports them;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location (country or city) is inferred;
  • device and app information, and how you interact with ads.

This is used for showing ads, ad measurement, frequency capping and preventing fraud. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland or a region with similar laws, the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before loading any ad, and you can change your choice any time in Settings › Privacy choices for ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Usage and crash reports (Google Firebase)

  • Firebase Analytics records app events such as "photo saved", which form it was for (for example "SSC photo"), which extra was used and whether an ad was shown. It also collects an app-instance ID, device information (model, Android version, language) and an approximate location from your IP address. Events never contain your photos, names or file names. Google keeps this data for 2 months.
  • Firebase Crashlytics sends a report when the app crashes: the technical details of the crash, device information and an installation ID. Google keeps crash reports for 90 days.

We use this only to understand which features are used and to fix problems. See Privacy and security in Firebase.

7. Children

The app is meant for people making photos for official forms and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Your choices

  • Change your ad consent in Settings › Privacy choices for ads (where offered).
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google or privacy settings.
  • Uninstall the app to delete everything it keeps on your phone. Photos you saved stay in your gallery until you delete them.
  • Questions or a data request: email neilbhowmik05@gmail.com. Because there is no account, include the date you used the app so we can help.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Important changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
